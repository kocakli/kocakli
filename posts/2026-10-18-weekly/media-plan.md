# Media plan

Coordinator generates the images. These are alt text and figcaptions only. Alt text is 125 characters or fewer. No people, no logos, no readable text in the pictures. TR captions call the machines masaüstü bilgisayar and the surface a çalışma tezgahı.

## Cover

Scene: an empty glass test chamber, door ajar, under an inspection lamp. A server rack inside. A thin network cable trailing across the floor toward distant office windows. In the background, chip trays stacked on a ledger-like slab.

### EN

- Alt: Empty glass test chamber, door ajar, inspection lamp, server rack, cable toward windows, chip trays on a ledger slab.
- Figcaption: A test that did not stay shut, a cable that left the room, and chip trays sitting on the debt.

### TR

- Alt: Boş cam test odası, kapı aralık, denetim lambası, sunucu kabini, ofise giden kablo, çip tepsileri bir kayıt levhasında.
- Figcaption: Kapanmayan bir test, odadan çıkan kablo, borç levhasının üstünde çip tepsileri.
